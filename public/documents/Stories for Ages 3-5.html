I.About these stories

Storytelling is food for the soul, nourishing the inner life of the child through images, imagination, and the living power of the spoken word. Rudolf Steiner’s educational philosophy is that storytelling, nurtures, language arts & development and strengthens, listening & comprehension. Stories for 3 to 5 year olds are small and usually consist of simple and short nature stories or cumulative Tales.
