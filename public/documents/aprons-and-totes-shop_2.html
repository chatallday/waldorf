II.Made with Care

A few words about how these will be made and what to expect when they're ready.

Materials

  • Natural fibres only — heavyweight cotton canvas for the totes, linen-cotton blends for the aprons, organic cotton where available. No synthetics, no polyester linings.
  • Undyed or naturally-coloured base cloth — the warm, slightly varied colour of unbleached natural fibre, which ages beautifully and softens with use.
  • Water-based, non-toxic printing inks — in the warm earth-tone palette of the directory, and in full colour for the watercolour-based designs.
  • Real stitching — double-stitched seams, reinforced handles on the totes, sturdy ties on the aprons. Built to last years, not seasons.

The original paintings

Most of the tote designs come from original paintings — the same gentle Waldorf painting technique used in classrooms across the world. They are printed in full colour onto natural canvas, so the softness of the original is carried through. No two prints are exactly alike; the watercolour's natural variation comes through in every batch.

What to expect

  • Small batches — twenty to fifty of each design at a time, then sometimes a wait before the next batch
  • Hand-printed onto real canvas, so each piece has small natural variations — part of the charm
  • Price range: aprons around $45–60, tote bags around $25–35 (final pricing announced at launch)
  • Shipping worldwide; postage at cost

Why these — and nothing else

The directory is not a shop. Almost everything we recommend — the puppets, the wooden toys, the wool, the silk, the dyes, the books, the cookbooks, the fonts — comes from other makers and other shops, all linked from the relevant guides. Those makers deserve your custom, and we send you to them gladly.

We make and sell these aprons and totes for one reason: they are the kinds of small, useful, beautiful things we wanted ourselves, and could not always find. Selling them keeps a small piece of the directory funded by craft rather than only by subscription. That feels right.

A small collection. Made by hand. Made to last. Made with the same care we hope you find in every page of the directory itself.
