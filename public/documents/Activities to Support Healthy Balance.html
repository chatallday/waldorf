Activities to Support Healthy Balance

Waldorf Education Resource · Lifecycle and Development · Health & Balance
Movement and sensory activities to support healthy balance and coordination in Waldorf education. This resource provides practical movement activities designed to support children's developing sense of balance and coordination. In Waldorf education, balance is understood not merely as a physical skill but as a foundational capacity that supports cognitive, emotional, and social development. Key Activities Include: - Beam walking and balance paths - One-footed standing games - Rhythmic movement with props - Sensory integration exercises - Outdoor nature balance activities These activities support the child's developing vestibular and proprioceptive systems, fostering confidence and body awareness.
