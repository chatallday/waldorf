About us

The Waldorf Path was woven together by three women — Romy, a certified early-childhood Waldorf educator; Elody, a soon-to-be Waldorf handwork teacher; and Nina, a mother whose three children grew up in the warmth of the Waldorf home and school. We come from different paths and different places, but we cherish the same deep conviction: that the wisdom living in Waldorf education deserves to be found, loved, and carried forward by as many families as possible. The Directory is our gift to your Waldorf path.
