I.About these stories

Storytelling is food for the soul, nourishing the inner life of the child through images, imagination, and the living power of the spoken word. Rudolf Steiner’s educational philosophy is that storytelling, nurtures, language arts & development and strengthens, listening & comprehension. Stories for 5-7 year olds are longer and are oftentimes folk tales and fairy tales.
