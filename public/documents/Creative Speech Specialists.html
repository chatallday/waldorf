I.What it is

Creative Speech, also called Speech Formation or Sprachgestaltung, is the art of shaping spoken language. Rudolf Steiner and Marie Steiner began it.

Speech is a pathway into the language arts and a journey of development. In Waldorf, the human voice is regarded as the primary instrument through which language rhythm and sound come alive. Children are invited to experience the living qualities of speech, not only through words, but through melody rhythm and sound

Steiner said: “The sounds themselves are our teachers — they will be the best teachers we will ever have.”

Quick facts

  • Taught in: Waldorf schools, Grades 1–12, and teacher training
  • Used for: language arts and inner development
  • Open to: teachers, actors, parents, anyone who works with the spoken word
A good first readDebra Spitulnik wrote an essay called “Developing the Art of Imagination through Creative Speech.” Many teachers recommend it as an introduction. You can find it at centerforanthroposophy.org.

II.Where to train

There is a full professional training in North America, one in the UK and one in Australia.

⭐ USA · Chestnut Ridge, New York

Steiner School of Speech Arts

This school is at the Threefold Community in Chestnut Ridge, and it is the main professional training in creative speech in North America. The full course takes four years and is in person. They also run one-week intensives that anyone can join, whatever their level.

Helen Lubin started “Speech Arts in Waldorf Schools in North America” in 1994. She trained at the Novalis School for Speech and Drama in Germany and at the Goetheanum in Switzerland. She taught at the School for Pedagogical and Therapeutic Speech in Dornach from 1989 to 1991, then at Rudolf Steiner College from 1992 to 2016. Over the years she has visited more than 60 Waldorf schools in North America, over 250 school visits in all, and she also works as a therapeutic speech practitioner.

Barbara Renold trained at the Goetheanum and at the Harkness Studio in Sydney. She taught speech and drama at Sunbridge College from 1983 to 2007. She directed two complete cycles of Steiner’s four mystery dramas, and the second ended with the Threefold Mystery Drama Festival in 2014. That was the first time one cast had performed all four plays at one conference in English.

Jennifer Kleinbach has a speech formation diploma from the Speech School of North America and a eurythmy diploma from Spring Valley. She teaches at the School of Eurythmy, at Sunbridge and in the Fiber Craft Studio, and she speaks for the Eurythmy Spring Valley ensemble.

info@steinerspeecharts.org · steinerspeecharts.org

⭐ UK · West Sussex, England

Artemis School of Speech and Drama

Artemis is the main speech and drama training in the UK. It used to be called the Speech School. Christopher Garvey has been Principal since 1990. He studied at Bristol Old Vic Drama School before he trained in speech formation. People say it is the longest-running Steiner-inspired speech training in Britain.

speechanddrama.uk

Australia · Sydney

Harkness Studio (Mechthild Harkness)

People call this the leading creative speech studio in Australasia. The training takes four years. Year 1 is epic poetry, year 2 is drama, year 3 is lyric poetry, and year 4 covers all three. Eurythmy, Greek gymnastics, performance and improvisation are part of it. Graduates have gone on to teach at the London School of Speech Formation and elsewhere, including in China.

Contact through Steiner Education Australia: steinereducation.edu.au

III.Mentors

Speech artists who mentor teachers and students.

⭐ Freeport, Maine

Craig Giddens

The Goetheanum has recognized Craig as a speech artist since 1978. He has worked as a Waldorf class teacher, speech teacher and mentor for more than 50 years, in the USA and Europe. He helped found the Center for Anthroposophy in New Hampshire and the Speech School of North America. He teaches at Sound Circle Center in Seattle and in the Waldorf High School Teacher Training in New Hampshire, and he currently mentors teachers at Maine Coast Waldorf School.

In 2022 he and Daniel Stokes started a four-year speech training, online and in person. Sound Circle calls it the first training of its kind in North America. It treats speech as a path of self and human development, and it is meant especially for teachers.

soundcirclecenter.org

⭐ USA

Daniel Stokes

Daniel has worked in the Waldorf movement since 1975. He was a class teacher for 24 years, and he brings a love of the spoken word into the classroom. He has taught courses in poetry, storytelling and drama at Waldorf training centers across the U.S. With Craig Giddens he launched the four-year speech training at Sound Circle in 2022.

soundcirclecenter.org

⭐ Tempe, Arizona, and online

Kim Snyder-Vine

Kim teaches creative speech, hygienic speech and drama, to children and to adults. She holds the four-year Goetheanum-approved diploma from the London School of Speech Formation, which she finished in 1983. Since then she has taught, performed and consulted in private Waldorf schools, Waldorf-inspired charter schools, teacher training institutes and artistic training centres around the world.

She helped found Portal Productions in London and Alchymia Theatre in Chicago. As a speaker she has performed with Ashdown Eurythmy (UK), The London Eurythmy Group, the Midwest Eurythmy Group in Chicago, Eurythmeum Stuttgart and Lemniscate Arts / Eurythmy Spring Valley in Chestnut Ridge, New York. She used to be on the faculty at Sophia’s Hearth in Keene, New Hampshire. These days she teaches private students online.

IV.Teachers and speech artists

USA

Debra Spitulnik

Debra teaches creative speech at Washington Waldorf School, at the Center for Anthroposophy in New Hampshire and in the Waldorf Teacher Education Programme at Antioch University New England. She trained with Geoff Norris of Voicing the Silence.

centerforanthroposophy.org

California, USA

Christine Burke

Christine is a Logodynamics practitioner and a creative speech artist. She gives workshops at BACWTT, including the yearly “Art of Speech” day, and she travels to give them elsewhere. Her audiences include teachers, teacher trainees, actors, parents and business people. She also visits Waldorf schools, teacher trainings and Christian Communities. She is a member of AASA.

bacwtt.org

International

Geoff Norris, Voicing the Silence

Geoff has developed what he calls the “Archetype Technique,” a modular professional training rooted in anthroposophy. It builds on the work of Rudolf and Marie Steiner, together with Michael Chekhov, Viola Spolin and Rudolf Laban. You can earn a Goetheanum-recognised speech diploma through it. He calls the whole project an “Alchemical Theatre of Transformation,” a theatre of the future.

speechanddramastudio.com

Online

Séamus Maynard, EduCareDo Speech & Drama

Séamus runs Speech and Drama as Living Arts, a year-long online foundation course inspired by Rudolf Steiner and Michael Chekhov. Each month there is one exercise to do with your body. Waldorf teachers, artists and anyone who wants to go deeper into artistic work can join. He trained at Guildhall in London, studied the Michael Chekhov Technique, and co-founded Shakespeare Alive!. He studied speech formation with Christy MacKaye Barnes and with Christopher Garvey at Artemis.

educaredo.org

Australia

Dawn Langman

Dawn is a speech formation artist who is working to bring speech formation together with Michael Chekhov’s approach to acting. Other people in the field often point to her as a leading voice where Steiner’s speech arts meet modern performance.

Contact through Steiner Education Australia: steinereducation.edu.au

V.The association

USA · International

Association for Anthroposophic Speech Arts (AASA)

AASA is the professional association for speech artists trained in the anthroposophic tradition. It has a member directory, articles and professional development. Helen Lubin, Christine Burke, Kim Snyder-Vine, Diane Caracciolo and Anja Macmurchie are among the members, along with many others.
