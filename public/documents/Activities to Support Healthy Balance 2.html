Activities to Support Healthy Balance 2

Waldorf Education Resource · Lifecycle and Development · Health & Balance
Additional movement and sensory activities to support healthy balance and coordination in Waldorf education. This second volume provides advanced balance activities building on the foundational exercises. These activities are suitable for older children and those who have mastered the basic balance exercises. Advanced Activities Include: - Dynamic balance on uneven surfaces - Partner balance exercises - Balance with eyes closed (advanced proprioception) - Rhythmic jumping and landing sequences - Integration with academic learning (balance while reciting, counting) These activities further refine the child's vestibular system and support the integration of movement with cognitive processes.
