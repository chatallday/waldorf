III.Dolls and Handmade Makers

  • Heidi Hilscher DollsGermany
    Made by hand in the Waldorf doll-making tradition, with careful detail from the dresses to the faces. The expressions are kept simple on purpose, so children can fill in the rest. Sold through Waldorf toy shops and The Wooden Wagon.
  • Nanchen DollsGermany
    A companion brand to Heidi Hilscher, with handmade dolls in the same tradition. Sold through Waldorf toy shops worldwide.
  • Nathalie de Saint-Hilaire (the2giftedhands)Arizona, USA
    Doll maker. Also works under the name the2giftedhands.
  • DollbelgeBelgium
    Sells doll making kits, such as the Sunshine Doll Making Kit for a cuddle doll.
  • Wood Pecker FactoryRomania, Etsy
    Hand-crafted wooden animals, trees, flowers, princesses and knights, in a style close to Ostheimer and Holztiger. Sea animals, woodland, farm, African animals, birds and dinosaurs. Based in Bucharest. Etsy: WoodPeckerFactory
  • Waldorf WorldUkraine, Etsy
    Needle-felted gnomes, fairies, fairy houses, animals, fruit, vegetables and birthday crowns, finished with pine cones, moss, snail shells and beading. Based in Dnipro. Ships internationally. Etsy: WaldorfWorld

    Waldorf-inspired animals and trees carved from alder, finished with non-toxic water-based paints, linseed oil and wax. Based in Novosibirsk. Ships to Europe and America. Etsy: GreenTaiga

IV.Skin Tone Crayons and Pencils

Children should be able to draw themselves, their families and their friends in their own colours.

Traditional "flesh" crayons covered only one skin tone. From Grade 1 on, children draw and paint people in their main lesson books, so having the right colours matters. These sets cover a much wider range.

  • We Are Colorful (Mudpuppy)USA, ages 3+
    18 wax crayons across the full range of skin tones, in a reusable tin. Meets CPSIA, ASTM and CE safety standards. Designed by Courtney Ahn. Sold at mudpuppy.com, Amazon and toy retailers.
  • Faber-Castell World Colors EcoPencilsGermany/USA, ages 5+
    27 coloured pencils, including 3 duo-tone pencils that give 6 blendable skin tone shades. Developed with makeup artists, and made from certified sustainable wood. Sold at fabercastell.com and art supply shops. The Black Edition has 12 skin tone pencils for use on dark paper.
  • Lyra Skin Tone Coloured PencilsGermany
    From the maker of the Super Ferby pencils that Waldorf schools have used for decades. Sold at the Cincinnati Waldorf School store (cincinnatiwaldorfschool.org) and selected Waldorf art suppliers.
  • Stockmar skin tone blocksGermany
    Several shades in the Assorted and Supplement sets work for skin tones. For the widest range, add the ochre, caramel, sienna and umber blocks. Used for portraits in main lesson books. Sold by Waldorf art suppliers.
  • Derwent Coloursoft SkintonesUK, Grades 6+
    A soft, blendable pencil set made for skin tone work. Popular with older students for portraits and figures in main lesson books. Sold at derwent.com and art supply shops.
