V.The four arcs

On the chart, a colored arc runs from the virtue of a higher sense down to the virtue of a bodily sense. Each higher quality has its root in a bodily one.

Reading the arcsThe yellow arc is the shortest and the blue arc is the widest. It starts at Faithfulness at the top and ends at Trust and Selflessness at the bottom.

VI.Color key

These are the colors each sense is written in on the original chart.

  • Yellow: Hearing, Warmth, Balance
  • Red: Word, Self-Movement
  • Purple: Thought, Life / Well-Being
  • Indigo: I-Being of the Other, Touch
  • Light green: Sight
  • Dark green: Taste
  • Blue: Smell
