Everything Waldorf in One Place

The Waldorf Directory is your trusted guide to the world of Waldorf education, thoughtfully bringing together everything Waldorf in one convenient place. Whether you are a parent, teacher, homeschooler, caregiver, or simply curious about the Waldorf approach, you’ll find carefully curated categories, resources, and guides designed to support your journey. Explore educational materials by grade level, discover recommended books and handwork supplies, find trusted artisans and creators, locate schools, mentors, therapists, and training programs, and learn where to buy authentic Waldorf-inspired products. Our growing collection of resources helps you spend less time searching and more time nurturing meaningful learning, creativity, and connection. From early childhood through high school, The Waldorf Directory makes finding the right resources simple, beautiful, and accessible — a real time saver for busy parents, teachers, and caregivers.

about us

The Waldorf Path was woven together by three women — Romy, a certified early-childhood Waldorf educator; Elody, a soon-to-be Waldorf handwork teacher; and Nina, a mother whose three children grew up in the warmth of the Waldorf home and school. We come from different paths and different places, but we cherish the same deep conviction: that the wisdom living in Waldorf education deserves to be found, loved, and carried forward by as many families as possible. The Directory is our gift to your Waldorf path.
