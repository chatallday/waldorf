All songs in the Waldorf kindergarten are pentatonic, sung in the "mood of the fifth." Rudolf Steiner recommended the pentatonic scale for the first seven years, as it gives the young child an open, boundless musical space — free from the tonal gravity of major and minor keys. The seasonal nature table accompanies each song cycle through the year.

Authors of English Waldorf Circle Songs & Resources

Key figures who have created, collected and published circle songs, songbooks and movement resources for English-speaking Waldorf kindergartens

Circle Song Authors & Educators
Circle Time Leader

Nancy Foster

Acorn Hill Waldorf Kindergarten, Silver Spring, MD · WECAN

Long-time teacher at Acorn Hill Waldorf Kindergarten and leading figure in WECAN teacher education. Her circle time collections are among the most widely used in English-speaking Waldorf kindergartens worldwide.

  • Let Us Form a Ring (Acorn Hill Anthology)
  • Dancing As We Sing
  • The Mood of the Fifth (editor)
Circle Time Author

Laurie Clark

Denver Waldorf School · Colorado, USA · WECAN

Lead kindergarten teacher at The Denver Waldorf School with 27 years of Waldorf kindergarten experience. Mentors Waldorf teachers across North America and presents internationally. Her books with Nancy Blanning are essential circle time resources in the English-speaking Waldorf world — described as "a book of genius and a gift to young children."

  • Movement Journeys and Circle Adventures, Vol. 1 (with Nancy Blanning, WECAN, 2006)
  • Movement Journeys and Circle Adventures, Vol. 2 (with Nancy Blanning, WECAN, 2016)
Songbook Author

Betty Jones

Waldorf educator · USA

Author of the most beloved all-in-one Waldorf circle resource for families and teachers. Her seasonal treasury is the first book recommended to parents entering the Waldorf world.

  • A Child's Seasonal Treasury
Songbook Author

Mary Thienes Schunemann

Waldorf musician & educator · USA

Completed a full series of pentatonic songbooks with CDs before her death. Her work brought pentatonic singing into many Waldorf-inspired homes that had no prior musical training.

  • Sing a Song of Seasons (with CD)
  • Lavender's Blue Dilly Dilly (with CD)
  • This is the Way We Wash-a-Day
Composer

Bronja Zahlingen

1912–2000 · Austrian-born Waldorf kindergarten teacher

Dedicated her life to creating and collecting circle games, finger games, songs, puppet plays and verses for Waldorf kindergartens. Her collected work is a rare and lasting gift to early childhood education.

  • A Lifetime of Joy (WECAN Books)
Music Theory

Jennifer Aulie

Waldorf music educator · Wynstones Press / WECAN

Author of the introductory essays on "music in the mood of the fifth" found in the Wynstones seasonal series (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Spindrift, Gateways). Key voice in explaining pentatonic music for Waldorf teachers.

  • Introductions to Wynstones seasonal series
  • Gateways (co-editor with Margret Meyerkort)
Composer

Ilian Willwerth

Waldorf musician · WECAN Books

Composed original songs in the mood of the fifth for young children.

  • Merrily We Sing
  • Let's Dance & Sing (arr. Kundry Willwerth)
